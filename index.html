Storyshot

Real web apps, made into videos by an agent

Each video below started as a one-paragraph request to an AI coding agent with the Storyshot skill. The agent looked at the site, wrote the storyboard, rendered, checked the frames and fixed what it found. Every example comes with its storyboard, so you can copy it, change the URL and make your own: npx -y storyshot setup claude (or cursor, codex, gemini, vscode, windsurf, cline)

Voices are free and local: Kokoro for English, Qwen3-TTS 0.6B CustomVoice (voice dylan) for the Chinese lessons, and Qwen3-TTS (voice aiden) for the Spanish one. Title and section cards are HyperFrames animations (hyperframes: true in the storyboard). Sites shown belong to their owners and are used as public examples. Notes for each video say how long it took, what went wrong and what the agent thought of the result.
