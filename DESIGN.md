# A personal screening room

The brief: an artistic, story-led bilingual home for boheling's films, with the warmth of a hobby site. The existing 23 films and production records supply the content. The tool pitch moves into About.

Newsreader for English display type, Noto Serif SC for Chinese display type, native sans-serif for body and controls. Body lines stay below 65 characters.

## Review before building

Rejected a dark cinema landing page and a cream editorial grid: both could fit almost any portfolio. A cool lavender sketchbook, three actual science/history stills and the films' own production notes make this specific. The collage is the one expressive gesture. No sales metrics, invented dates, decorative chapter numbers, repeated card shells or automatic carousel.

## Behavior

The whole interface, film titles and summaries switch between Chinese and English, with a saved preference. Films and source documents retain their original language. Search both languages. Preserve playback, storyboards, notes, articles, category anchors and pitch. Use native dialog, visible focus, reduced motion, mobile layouts, lazy images and click-to-load video.

The original checkout has an unrelated untracked film. Work stays in an isolated checkout on codex/artful-bilingual-gallery; existing media and production documents are unchanged.
